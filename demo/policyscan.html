Does this website do what its privacy policy says?

PolicyScan reads a privacy policy, checks which trackers the site really loads, and gives it a score out of 10 with the reasons behind every point.

Try a sample site:

Text-only scans can't check the live site, so the Policy vs. practice category is skipped and the score is scaled to 10.

    This is a front-end prototype for the capstone proposal. The three sample sites and their scan results are fictional. Pasted text is scored in your browser with simple keyword rules that stand in for the trained NLP model. PolicyScan is informational and not legal advice.
